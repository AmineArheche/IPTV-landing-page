# Troubleshooting & Technical FAQ

## Common Setup Issues

### 1. How do I setup IPTV on Amazon Fire TV Stick?
1. Install **Downloader** app from the Amazon Appstore.
2. Enter the IPTV player download URL (e.g., TiviMate or IPTV Smarters Pro).
3. Open the app and select **Add Playlist (Xtream Codes API)**.
4. Input your Server URL, Username, and Password provided in your welcome email.

### 2. Stream Buffering or Freezing
- Ensure your internet download speed is at least **25 Mbps for HD** and **50 Mbps for 4K**.
- Connect via Ethernet cable if Wi-Fi signal is weak or unstable.
- Clear app cache in device settings or reboot router.

### 3. Audio / Video Out of Sync
- In your player settings, switch hardware decoding from **HW** to **SW** (Software decoding) or vice versa.
- Check playlist format (HLS vs MPEG-TS).
