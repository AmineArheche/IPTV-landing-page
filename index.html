Frequently Asked Questions

What devices are compatible?

Our service works on Smart TVs (Samsung, LG, Android TV), FireStick, MAG boxes, smartphones (iOS/Android), tablets, computers, and more. If it can connect to the internet, it can stream!

How fast does activation take?

Your account is activated instantly after payment. You'll receive your login credentials via email within minutes and can start streaming immediately.

What internet speed do I need?

We recommend at least 10 Mbps for HD streaming and 25 Mbps for 4K content. Our anti-freeze technology optimizes playback even on slower connections.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes! Monthly subscriptions can be cancelled anytime with no penalties. Annual and lifetime plans are non-refundable but you retain access for the full period.

Do you offer a free trial?

We offer a 24-hour money-back guarantee. If you're not satisfied with the service within the first 24 hours, contact support for a full refund.

Is customer support available?

Yes! Our support team is available 24/7 via live chat and email. We typically respond within 15 minutes during business hours.
